Dear blog,

I think it's time to end it all.

I was sitting on the side of the road, barking at the maillady like a good boy; she's so beautiful, I just can't help myself. Finally, after months, she talked to me, but not how I'd always dreamed it would be.

She said, very angrily, "what you stupid dog?! What could you possibly want with me?!"

Her eyes were so dreamy. It was then that I decided she would be the 3rd person in the world to learn I could talk. I said to her sensually "Nothing my love, just your beautiful smile in my paws."

She screamed. She called me a freak. She whipped out her pepper spray and tried to shoot it at me, but it was facing backwards and she hit herself in the eyes. As I tried to lick the pepper from her face (it's tasty) she started sobbing and begging me to go.

I didn't know what to do.

I think she hates me now. I don't know how I can go on.
